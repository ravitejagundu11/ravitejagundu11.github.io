01. About

I'm a software engineer in Seattle, WA. I judge my work by what changes for the people who use it: less time spent on a task, fewer errors, and a product that keeps working when traffic spikes.

At AWS, I built a cable-management workflow for data-center install engineers. A cable change that took about 2 minutes in the old system now takes under 15 seconds. The new APIs also caught stale edits (20% of edits during my internship) that would otherwise have silently overwritten someone else's work. Before launch, selected users tried it in pre-production behind a feature flag.

At Sureify, I spent four years on insurance products that carriers such as Amica, AAA, Kuvare and Navy Mutual put in front of their own customers. A job that polled DocuSign for signing status was crashing the server under heavy load, so I replaced it with a webhook pipeline. Signing status now updated instantly, other teams' apps stopped showing long loading spinners after signing, and peak server load dropped 30%. When a client deadline depended on a PDF feature that Go's libraries couldn't handle, I built a Python service on AWS Lambda that met the deadline without buying a paid license.

I also work closely with the people around the code. At Sureify I clarified requirements with project managers, gathered needs from teams adopting the e-signature service, demoed new features to customers, and mentored two junior engineers starting from their onboarding. I added integration tests that cut bugs reaching QA by 20% by catching issues early in development.
